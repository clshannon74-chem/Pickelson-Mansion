Forensic Investigation Unit • PM-001

The Pickelson Mansion Incident

Preliminary case file • Suspicious explosion • Suspected homicide

Incident Report

Initial notification • Approximately 2:45 a.m.

A forensic investigator was summoned to Pickelson Mansion following reports of a violent explosion. Earlier that evening, the Pickelsons had hosted a private dinner party. Seven victims were reportedly recovered, but their identities could not immediately be established.

Investigators believe one person was murdered before the explosion. It is not yet known whether the explosion was accidental, deliberate, or connected to the suspected homicide.

Officer Dunkin, the first responding officer, supplied limited details. A neighbour provided information about the people connected to the party. These personal accounts have not been independently verified.

Investigator's assignment: Complete the percent composition calculations for all 11 compounds in class. Have your teacher check and sign your work. Then you may immediately begin matching the forensic evidence and writing your report, in class or at home. Unfinished calculations must be handed in to your teacher at the end of class; they may not be completed at home.

Outstanding questions

Which compounds match the recovered samples? Which individuals can be identified from the evidence? Who was murdered before the explosion, and what explanation best fits the findings?

The initial account describes seven victims, but the laboratory log contains eight numbered evidence records. Do not assume every record is a separately confirmed fatality.
